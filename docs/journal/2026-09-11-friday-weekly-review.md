## Hypotheses to track — do not treat as conclusions
1. Total caffeine / stimulant load, especially when coffee and Celsius overlap.
2. Timing of caffeine relative to the afternoon activation period and bedtime.
3. Under-fueling from one-meal-a-day patterns on demanding workdays.
4. Hydration / electrolytes.
5. Cannabis or vape timing and its relationship to subjective anxiety/restlessness or heart rate.
6. Long uninterrupted screen / work blocks.
7. Sleep duration and consistency the night before.
8. Lack of movement during the workday.

## 7-day experiment
